01 manifesto

Most sites are furniture. I build rooms you walk into — with light, distance and a floor that holds.

I work alone on purpose. One pair of hands from the first sketch to the deploy, so the thing that ships is the thing we agreed on.

No template shops, no page builders, no design system for four screens. Semantic HTML, one stylesheet, one script — and the browser does the rest.

If it does not open in under a second on a phone in a bad neighbourhood, it is not finished.
